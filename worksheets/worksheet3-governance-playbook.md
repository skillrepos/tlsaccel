### Back at work: Box 1 — Inventory (where is AI used today?)

**Optional, for back at work.** List two or three AI tools already in use where you work. For each one, give the **tool and what it is used for**, its **owner** (the one person answerable for it; "nobody yet" is a real answer), and the **data it touches** (what information it can see). **Autonomy tier** is how much the tool does on its own. **1 assist:** AI drafts, a person does the work. **2 review:** AI prepares the action, a named person approves it. **3 autonomous:** AI acts, and people check afterwards.

**Keep it general.** Give owners as job titles, not names. Describe the kind of data ("customer emails"), never the data itself. Describe unapproved use as a habit ("people paste customer emails into personal chatbots"), without saying who does it. No lab step asks you to paste this box into an AI tool, so do not.

### Back at work: Box 3 — Approval path

The approval path is how a new AI idea gets a yes or a no: it is scored on Worksheet 1, someone checks it for risk, and an answer comes back within a set number of days.

| Box | Needed in the lab? | What goes in it | Example | Your answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Risk check by** | Optional | Who looks at a new AI idea for risk before it is approved: one person or a small group | The review group (Box 6) | |
| **Target turnaround (days)** | Optional | How many days someone with a new AI idea should wait for a yes or a no | 5 | |

### Back at work: Box 4 — Vendor checks

The questions you will put to every company that sells you an AI tool. Tick the ones you want on your standard list.

- ☐ Do you train on our data?
- ☐ Where is our data stored?
- ☐ How do we leave? (how we get our data out and stop using the tool)
- ☐ Do you support MCP / standard integration? (MCP is the common standard for connecting AI tools to your systems)

### Back at work: Box 5 — Incident path

An incident is AI going wrong: it leaks information, makes something up that gets used, or takes a wrong action. The incident path says who gets the first call.

The audit trail is the record of what your AI tools did, kept so that someone can check afterwards: what gets logged, who reviews it, and how long the logs are kept.
