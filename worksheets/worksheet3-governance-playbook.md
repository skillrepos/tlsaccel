# Worksheet 3 — One-Page Governance Playbook + 30/60/90
*Enterprise Leader AI Accelerator · Labs 7–8*

**What this is:** two things on one sheet. A **governance playbook**: six boxes that set out how AI is used where you work — what is in use, what is allowed, who approves new ideas, and who gets the call when something goes wrong. And a **30 / 60 / 90**: what you will do in the next 90 days, with dates. You start both today and finish them back at work. Each box says what goes in it and gives an example from a made-up company.

Fill it in on the worksheets page, which saves what you type on your own device: https://skillrepos.github.io/tlsaccel/#ws3
